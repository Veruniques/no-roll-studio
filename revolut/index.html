I design commercial products end to end, on iOS, Android and web, in places where a tap has to earn something and a wrong one means a refund. Here’s the short route through my work, in the order I’d show it in the room.

Discovery to delivery, on all three platforms

Fare Families at Kiwi.com: from the first sketch to shipping with Ryanair and Air France-KLM, on web, iOS and Android, including refunds and the customer-service tool.

Decisions I can defend

One feature, from the ticket and benchmark to design critique and what we took forward.

Details that earn money

Co-led Ancillaries at Kiwi.com, with a goal of €2 to €25 per booking and double the overall revenue. We A/B tested variants and kept what performed, using revenue, CX feedback, UX research and quantitative data.

Tested before building, reviewed after release

At Keboola I led interviews on prototypes before development, then post-release interviews and funnel reviews to see where users dropped off.

Design systems, more than once

Design systems for product and website at Keboola, a shared component set for web, apps and email at Kiwi.com, and a global design system at SinnerSchrader.

Clean craft, beyond the product

Typography, grids and visual systems, down to the websites I still design. I also prototype in code. This site is one.
